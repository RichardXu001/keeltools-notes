A zero-budget AI stack: four jobs, four free tiers

2026-10-20 · ~7 min

Subscription fatigue is real: buy the flagship in every slot and you clear $100 a month easily. But for most usage patterns, free tiers cover about seventy percent of the work.

The short version: fill four slots — chat, search, notes, automation — stay on free tiers everywhere, and pay for exactly one slot after two weeks of evidence. The stack costs $0; the upgrade decision waits for proof.

01 Four slots, four jobs

Each slot owns one job
SlotOwnsTypical free-tier candidates
ChatWriting, editing, asking, translatingChatGPT / Claude / Gemini free tiers
SearchCited retrieval and summariesPerplexity / NotebookLM
NotesKnowledge capture and linksObsidian / Notion / Capacities
AutomationSchedules and cross-app chainsself-hosted n8n / Make / Zapier free tiers

Chat is the only slot where the free tier genuinely hits a wall — message caps, model tiers and file limits bite. The other three are invisible for light-to-medium use.

02 A sample lightweight stack

Chat goes to the ChatGPT or Claude free tier (pick one; running both just splits your history). Search goes to Perplexity. Notes go to Obsidian — local files, zero lock-in, your data leaves with you. Automation starts on self-hosted n8n or Make's free tier.

For full profiles of everything in this stack — pricing, features, pros and cons — filter the main-site directory by “free”; building the shortlist takes five minutes.

03 When to break the free-tier rule

Three triggers

One: the same “out of quota” message three or more times in two weeks — that's a real wall, not a mood. Two: a tool becomes the first thing you open every day — high-frequency slots deserve the paid tier. Three: a missing capability blocks the workflow (API access, team sharing) — that's a feature gap, not a quota gap.

The counter-example

Buying a suite for “might need it someday” is the main cause of subscription fatigue. The point of the free stack is turning “might” into evidence: pay only for pain you've actually felt.

04 The upgrade path

When that day comes, most people's first payment is the chat slot (the ~$20 personal tier) and the second is automation (self-hosted n8n stays free; Make's paid tiers bill per task). Search and notes stay free longer than you'd expect.

If you keep one rule: run the $0 stack for two weeks, and pay only for pain that showed up. Subscriptions buy evidence, not potential.
